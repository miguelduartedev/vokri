## 4. Linguistic verification and explicitly deferred grammar

| Teaching decision | Finite verified forms / meaning | Boundary and source |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown | `keskusta → keskustaan / keskustassa / keskustasta` | To, in, from; do not teach universal noun-destination suffix generation |
| Shop/house source | `kauppa → kaupasta`, `talo → talosta` | Taught `-sta/-stä` recognition, not arbitrary source production |
| Station/stop source | `asemalle/asemalla/asemalta`, `pysäkille/pysäkillä/pysäkiltä` | A station or stop commonly follows external-place series; `-sta` is not a universal from-ending |
| Transport method | `bussilla`, `junalla` for controlled production; `metrolla`, `raitiovaunulla`, `taksilla` recognition | Means 'by' a vehicle; no general adessive grammar engine |
| Inside a bus | `bussissa` | Refers to being inside/on the bus, distinct from travelling **by** bus |
| Movement adverbs | `täällä/tänne`; `siellä/sinne` | Location vs movement-towards distinct; not interchangeable |
| Direction words | `oikea / vasen`; `oikealle / vasemmalle` | Basic dictionary directions vs finite direction forms in commands |
| Imperative commands | `Mene suoraan`; recognition-only `Tule tänne`, `Käänny oikealle/vasemmalle` | Fixed chunks, not a productive imperative table; kääntyä not added as a new production lemma |
| Recognition-only reach | `Miten pääsen keskustaan?` | Päästä is recognised, not conjugated productively |
| Third-person bus question | `Minne tämä bussi menee?` | Fixed recognition-only question; no full person table |
| Spoken casual | `takaisin → takas`; earlier `meen/tuun` | Optional recognition; standard written answers only |

### Source verification and limits

- **Aalto OpenLearning:** [Basic Finnish local cases: Mihin/Missä/Mistä](https://openlearning.aalto.fi/mod/page/view.php?id=17161) explicitly distinguishes `kahvilaan/kahvilassa/kahvilasta` and `pysäkille/pysäkillä/pysäkiltä`.
- **Uusi kielemme:** [Inside/on vs by bus](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/finnish-cases/location-cases/hyllylla-or-hyllyssa-poydassa-or-poydalla) directly distinguishes `bussissa` (location) from `bussilla` (travel method).
- **Uusi kielemme:** [Traffic vocabulary](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-lists/traffic-liikenne) gives `Menen kouluun bussilla`, `Matkustan junalla` and `Menen metrolla kotiin` in natural contexts.
- **Yle educational content:** [Places and directions](https://yle.fi/a/20-153572) lists `metrolla/junalla/bussilla/taksilla`, and [left/right directions](https://yle.fi/a/20-140731) distinguishes `vasen/oikea` from `vasemmalle/oikealle`.
- **Uusi kielemme:** [Asking for directions](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-phrases/asking-for-directions-in-finnish-tien-neuvominen) includes `Käänny oikealle`, `Käänny vasemmalle`, and `Mene suoraan eteenpäin`.
- **Uusi kielemme:** [Movement adverbs](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/word-types/adverbs/adverbs-of-location-taalla-kaikkialla-ylhaalla) gives `Tule tänne` and `Menen sinne` as directional expressions.
- **Uusi kielemme:** [Bus traffic phrases](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-lists/finnish-bus-traffic-phrases-bussipysakilla) identifies useful transport words and a fixed bus destination question.
- **Suomisanakirja:** [asema](https://www.suomisanakirja.fi/asema) and [pysäkki](https://www.suomisanakirja.fi/pys%C3%A4kki) give actual station/stop examples and forms.

These sources support particular Finnish words, forms and communicative distinctions. They are **not** a claim that a teacher or native reviewer certified all authored Vokri sentences, distractors or grading decisions. The original CSV examples/glosses are also not automatically teacher-approved.

## 9. QA corrections, limitations and risks

- **Validated:** Every one of the 28 Unit 8 lexical ranks matches the original CSV. All 19 production items have a first formal encounter and independently retrievable spelling task; all nine recognition-only items have authored comprehension and R01–R09 due Review records.
- **Validated:** Six Unit 8 lessons, **133 sequential LessonStep rows**, **101 required exercise records**, **12 matching boards with 27 pair decisions**, **16 due-Review variants**, **117 unique exercise IDs** and six lesson/assessment blocks, with additional capstone inside 8.6.
- **Fixed before PR:** D01 and D03 now score previously taught Unit 4 location words instead of Unit 8 movement grammar before its actual introduction.
- **Fixed before PR:** F18 reuses the real Unit 1 phrase ID `phrase.olen-slot`, not a fabricated `phrase.hello.olen`.
- **Fixed before PR:** The Unit 8.6 final three-line guidance was moved **before**, not after, the stage-capstone checkpoint.
- **Fixed before PR:** Removed an unused broad `grammar.getting-around.transport-lexicon` target; explicit transport nouns score their canonical VocabularyItems.
- **Cognitive load:** 8.5's 19 tasks and 8.6's 21 tasks are relatively heavy. Real timed novice testing and pause/resume may reveal a need to optimise presentation or split work inside the same lesson without introducing a seventh lesson.
- **Runtime dependency:** F21 requires three scoped deterministic inputs or a validated multiline matcher; default N1 must not destroy sentence boundaries. Wrong-versus-Almost semantic mapping is a **pre-existing** architecture issue; do not disguise a totally wrong answer as “Almost!”.
- **Answer fidelity:** The accepted lists intentionally restrict learner responses to taught forms. Any additional linguistically valid variant should be editorially reviewed and added explicitly, not guessed by an AI grader at runtime.
- **Native QA not completed:** No Expo build, real iOS/Android screen, VoiceOver/TalkBack, screen-reader focus test, reduced motion verification, Finnish keyboard/autocorrect test, actual time measurement, transit integration or spontaneous language assessment exists from this content PR.
- **Linguistic editorial:** Reference-backed grammar forms do not certify all newly authored Vokri prompts. Owner approval suffices internally; no external Finnish-teacher endorsement or measured CEFR result is claimed.
- **No unrelated change:** This authoring PR is scoped to Unit 8 and its coverage/implementation Markdown; it does not modify Units 1–7, Figma, original CSV or the scheduled Review code.
