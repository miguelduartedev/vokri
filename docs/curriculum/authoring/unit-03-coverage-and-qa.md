## 7. Linguistic verification and authored ambiguity checks

**Verified external facts, separate from newly authored Vokri content:**

1. [Kotus — Verbin persoonamuoto](https://kielitoimistonohjepankki.fi/ohje/verbin-persoonamuoto/): person agreement and omission of overt first/second-person subjects in standard Finnish.
2. [Aalto OpenLearning — Course grammar, Verb Types 1–5](https://openlearning.aalto.fi/mod/book/tool/print/index.php?id=12556): first-person `puhun`, `kysyn`, `tulen`, `menen`, and the warning that different verb types have distinct stems.
3. [Aalto — Type 2 irregular `tehdä`](https://openlearning.aalto.fi/mod/book/view.php?chapterid=402&id=7549): verifies `teen`, not `tehdän`.
4. [Aalto — Making verbs negative](https://openlearning.aalto.fi/mod/book/view.php?chapterid=771&id=12556): `en puhu`, `en ymmärrä`, same negative-verb pattern as Unit 2.
5. [Yle Oppiminen — Supisuomea, verb types](https://yle.fi/a/20-155972): standard `haluan`, `tulen`, `menen` and the fact that no single conversion rule covers all verbs.
6. [Uusi kielemme — Finnish verbtypes](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/consonant-gradation/the-finnish-verbtypes): independent readable conjugation reference for verb type 5 **tarvita → tarvitsen** and type 4 **haluta → haluan**. The original Aalto PDF was found, but its page-rendering verification was unavailable, so it is not cited as visual evidence.
7. [WordDive — Verbi + verbi](https://www.worddive.com/fi/grammar/suomen-kielioppi/6-verbit/6-4-verbi-verbi/): `haluan/voin +` dictionary-form second verbs in the taught contexts, **not** a rule permitting `tarvitsen mennä`.
8. [Kotus — En, et, ei](https://kielitoimistonohjepankki.fi/ohje/konjunktiot-eika-ei-eika-ja-ei/): negative verb forms and the independent `ei` response.
9. [Uusi kielemme — First 100 Finnish verbs](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-lists/your-first-100-finnish-verbs-finnish-for-beginners): supplementary S2 first-person checks, including `ymmärrän`, `tiedän`, `opiskelen`, `kysyn`, `vastaan`, `sanon`.
10. [Vokri original curated CSV](../vocabulary/finnish_2000_curated.csv): rank/lemma identity only, not certification of its AI-written example translations.

**Cross-checked rather than assumed:**

- The fourteen first-person teaching forms: `haluan, tarvitsen, tiedän, ymmärrän, voin, teen, menen, tulen, luen, kirjoitan, opiskelen, kysyn, vastaan, sanon`. Standard Finnish forms are used consistently; source support is stronger for some verbs than others. Final forms still warrant product-owner review in context.
- Negatives `en tiedä`, `en ymmärrä`, `en puhu` are valid. `en tiedän`, `en ymmärrän` and `en puhun` are never accepted as correct; they may appear as explicit errors with diagnostic feedback.
- Fixed-complement distinction: `Tarvitsen apua` uses supplied contextual `apua`; `Haluan puhua/Haluan mennä` and `Voin puhua/Voin tulla` use authored two-verb combinations. No unsupported `Tarvitsen mennä` generation.
- Going versus coming tasks supply a named viewpoint, and the final F15 explicitly refers to a friend waiting at their place. The choice of `mennä/tulla` must not be graded as globally unique in every real situation.
- `saada` and `pitää` are polysemous; Unit 3 scores only the one meaning actually supported by the teaching context.
- The eight lesson matching boards cover eighteen pairs, each with one authored counterpart. No artificial unique English glosses are invented solely to make otherwise ambiguous words match.
- N1 normalization is Unicode-aware and deterministic. Near misses retain truthful feedback, no fuzzy AI scoring. Optional Word Match and Flashcards never update the due Review queue.

**What wasn't verified:** real native keyboard and text scaling, independent S2 teacher certification, user learning outcomes, actual novice pacing, VoiceOver/TalkBack reading order, and whether every short situation feels authentic to all speakers. Those remain product/editorial QA tasks.
