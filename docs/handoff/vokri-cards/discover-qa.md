## Visual and accessibility checks

Compared the real screenshots with editable Finnish and Swedish landing frames, the featured and themed deck layouts, catalogue, search, topic results, word details, and dark and responsive variants. The implementation uses the foundation header with its active language chip, the cream or forest semantic surfaces, Newsreader display headings, and bundled Figma search, filter, close and collection-arrow assets. Counts, examples and forms come from the registry. Browsing rows open word details. The detail composition has a count or vocabulary card in place of illustrative study progress and enrolment actions. [Finnish Discover](https://www.figma.com/design/vJPxcNnHyCOAp57MBh46O7/Finnish-Learning-App?node-id=413-1444) · [Swedish Discover](https://www.figma.com/design/vJPxcNnHyCOAp57MBh46O7/Finnish-Learning-App?node-id=465-4855) · [Catalogue](https://www.figma.com/design/vJPxcNnHyCOAp57MBh46O7/Finnish-Learning-App?node-id=420-2476) · [Swedish detail](https://www.figma.com/design/vJPxcNnHyCOAp57MBh46O7/Finnish-Learning-App?node-id=465-5026)

- On the first Android pass, Gboard and the tab bar left the first search result below the visible content. The final layout hides tabs with the keyboard and compacts the header when vertical room is limited. Tapping juna with Gboard open reached its detail.
- Checked 360-wide Discover and 430-wide Swedish detail. At 390 × 720 and 160% system type, ammattikorkeakoulu wrapped across lines, its bilingual example scrolled, and the tab bar stayed accessible. [Narrow](screenshots/discover/fi-narrow.png) · [Wide](screenshots/discover/sv-wide.png) · [Large type](screenshots/discover/fi-large-short.png) · [Scrolled](screenshots/discover/fi-large-end.png)
- Inspected dark surfaces, borders, titles and row affordances on Discover, a collection and a detail. [Dark Discover](screenshots/discover/sv-dark.png) · [Dark deck](screenshots/discover/sv-dark-food.png) · [Dark detail](screenshots/discover/sv-dark-coffee.png)
- Exercised labelled controls through the native accessibility hierarchy, checked selected-state semantics in tests and exercised Android Back. Search has an accessible label, clear/close actions and a live result count. Controls use the shared 44-unit minimum touch target. Lists virtualize rows and keep canonical keys. Frame times, TalkBack speech output and VoiceOver were not measured.

iOS verification covered the production export. This Mac has Command Line Tools without an iOS Simulator runtime. Signed releases and physical devices remain unverified. The content remains an internal preview pending independent linguistic and release review.
